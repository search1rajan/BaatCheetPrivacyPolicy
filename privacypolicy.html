1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Baat Cheet may handle:

  • Account information: email address, display name, profile photo, and an account identifier provided by Firebase.
  • Chat information: messages, message timestamps, delivery or read status, and other information needed to operate chats.
  • Media: photos, videos, and documents that you choose to send. Media may be stored with Cloudinary, while related chat records may be stored with Firebase/Firestore.
  • Presence information: online/offline availability and last-active time, where those features are enabled.
  • Notification information: a device push-notification token and information needed to deliver notifications or support notification replies.
  • Device permissions: camera, photo-library, or video access only when you choose to use a related feature.
  • Feature information: if screenshot detection or one-time messages are enabled, the app may process related status information to provide those features.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • send and receive messages and media;
  • show presence and message status;
  • deliver notifications and support notification replies;
  • protect, maintain, and troubleshoot the app; and
  • respond to support requests.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Messages and media you send are visible to the other participants in the relevant chat. Service providers such as Firebase/Google and Cloudinary may process information to provide app services.

We do not claim that Baat Cheet uses end-to-end encryption. Do not send highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable sharing it with the other chat participant and having it processed by the services needed to operate the app.

4. Storage and Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the app’s features, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations. Deleting a message or media item may take time to propagate. Limited copies may remain in backups or technical records for a period of time.

We cannot guarantee removal of content that another user has already downloaded, saved, copied, or captured in a screenshot.

5. Deleting Your Data

If the app provides an account-deletion or “Delete my shared media” option, you can use that option to submit a deletion request. You may also contact us at support@example.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Deleting shared media may remove it from the chat for both participants. Read the confirmation shown in the app before proceeding. Some older uploads, backups, or copies may not be removable immediately.

6. Your Choices and Permissions

You can change camera, photo-library, and notification permissions in your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working. You can contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information where applicable.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child’s information was provided to the app, contact us at support@example.com to request its review or deletion. Before making the app available to children, review the age requirements and applicable laws for your audience.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
